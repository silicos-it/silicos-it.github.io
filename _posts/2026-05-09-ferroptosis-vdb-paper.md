Researchers at the KU Leuven and University of Antwerp contributed to a large international team studying ferroptosis inhibition as 
a therapeutic strategy against ischemia-reperfusion injury in transplantation and related conditions. Our contribution in this work,
which was published in the prestigious journal <a href="/assets/papers/ferroptosis-vdb-paper.pdf" download>Cell</a>
resulted in panel H of Figure 2 — a small but significant part of the study, however representing more than 
six months of umbrella sampling calculations performed on the European supercomputer LUMI.
