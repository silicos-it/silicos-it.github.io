Researchers at KU Leuven and the University of Antwerp contributed to a large international team studying ferroptosis inhibition as 
a therapeutic strategy against ischemia-reperfusion injury in transplantation and related conditions.
<a href="/assets/papers/ferroptosis-vdb-paper.pdf" download>Our contribution in this work</a>, which was published in the prestigious 
journal Cell resulted in panel H of Figure 2 — a small but significant part of the study, however representing more than 
six months of umbrella sampling calculations performed on the European supercomputer LUMI.
